// ABOUT

I'm a designer based in New York who has been freelancing for nearly a decade, largely self-teaching across different mediums and constantly finding new things to learn. My experience spans sports, fintech, healthcare, life sciences, wellness, fashion, and e-commerce, giving me the chance to work across a wide range of people, problems, and creative disciplines.

Outside of design, I'm usually moving or making something. I love baseball and softball, surf, inline skate, ice skate, draw, and compete in judo, with plans to eventually compete in BJJ. I love being outdoors and around communities built through shared interests, movement, and competition.

I'm currently building something novel for grappling, bringing together my interests in design, technology, and the sports I love. My path has also included some less conventional roles, from being a US Open ball person and Chelsea Piers skate guard to working on commercial shoots and even shoveling snow with the NYC Department of Sanitation.

At the core, I'm curious by nature and enjoy learning through doing. I want to create beautiful, functional things for the world I live in. Oh yeah! I also have a cat with a very dog like personality named Donatello, of course after the Ninja Turtle.

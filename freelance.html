// OVERVIEW

Things people hold, hang and walk past

Alongside product work I've freelanced as GENPUGDESIGN since 2018. This page collects the physical and print side of that work: packaging, posters, window signage and apparel graphics. Each piece had to work as a real object, at its real size, for a real audience.

// 01 / PACKAGING

Medusa king size rolling papers

MEDUSA SOLUTIONS, INC. · BOOKLET PACKAGING · 2024–25

A branded rolling paper booklet for Medusa, a cannabis discovery startup where I was the founding designer. The outside wraps the Medusa wordmark and serpent mark around a snake-scale pattern. When the booklet opens, a pair of illustrated eyes looks back at you, which plays on the "Turn to Stone" line.

FORMAT
King size booklet, 50 leaves
TEMPLATE
111 × 26 × 4.3 mm
PAPER
108 × 44 mm
ON-PACK COPY
Prop 65 warning, 21+ statement, FDA disclaimer, barcode
// MY PART
  • Designed the outside and inside artwork, including the scale pattern and eye illustration
  • Drew the outside and inside dielines with paper feed, sides and covers labeled and measured
  • Produced the perspective renders and assembled the tech pack for the manufacturer
// 02 / PRINT & CAMPAIGN

Turn to Stone posters

MEDUSA SOLUTIONS, INC. · CAMPAIGN · 2025

Posters for Medusa's Turn to Stone campaign, including the 4/20 launch push. Each one uses a strong duotone or gradient treatment on the photography so the series reads as one family, whether it shows up in a feed or on a wall.

// MY PART
  • Art directed the shoots the imagery came from, then designed and wrote the posters

// 04 / APPAREL

Medusa t-shirt concepts

MEDUSA SOLUTIONS, INC. · SKETCHBOOK · 09.10.24

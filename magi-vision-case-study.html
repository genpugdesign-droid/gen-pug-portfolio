// OVERVIEW

A computer-vision platform for combat-sports gyms

Magi Vision pairs computer vision with purpose-built gym hardware, giving coaches and athletes a way to see what's actually happening on the mat — in real time, without a camera crew or a manual tagging pipeline. As Co-Founder & CXO of Seriyoku Zenyo Labs, I own product and experience design end to end: the hardware's physical presence in the gym, the software layer coaches and athletes actually use, and the story we tell investors and early partners.

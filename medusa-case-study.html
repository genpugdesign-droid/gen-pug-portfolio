// OVERVIEW

Making cannabis discovery feel like a community

Medusa Solutions Inc. is a startup building a cannabis delivery and discovery platform for adults 21 and up in Arizona and New York City. Finding a dispensary means comparing distance, hours, deals, delivery options, payment methods, and a deep product catalog — strain, potency, effects, weight — across stores that all look the same online.

My job was to make that simple. The iOS app and website turn each dispensary into a community people can find, trust, and talk to, with the complexity tucked behind a map, clear badges, and filters that read like a conversation: "How far do you want your dispensary to be?" "How much you got?" The platform is live at medusa.so.

// MY ROLE

In the room from conception to launch

I joined Medusa at its conception as founding designer. Beyond building the brand identity, I led across every touchpoint that shaped the business — the brief that framed the launch, the apparel and shoots that carried the brand before there was product to sell, and the app and website customers would actually use. My through-line was turning complex ideas into something a customer understands in a glance, and showing up in person to make it happen — including flying from NYC to Miami to lead a shoot.

// PERSONAS

Who Medusa is for

Medusa's customers are adults 21 and up in Arizona and New York City. These proto-personas are composites built from that audience to guide design decisions. They're three different lives with one shared need: cannabis that fits around them, whether the barrier is mobility or a schedule that never lines up with store hours.

// WEBSITE REDESIGN

A web front door for the marketplace

The redesigned desktop site puts discovery first. Search for products or dispensaries sits at the top next to your location. Category pills cover flower through tinctures. A persistent sidebar holds Home, Map, Match, Strains, Profile, Favorites, Orders, and Learn. The home page opens on the brand, then helps people decide: Medusa Match offers collections to explore tonight (Popular, Evening Picks, New Arrivals). Nearby Dispensaries lists licensed shops within 25 miles, with distance and live-menu badges. Visitors can switch between light and dark appearance.

The live desktop home at medusa.so.

EARLIER EXPLORATIONS

Before the redesign settled on a sidebar and Match, early web flows explored a deals-led home screen and launch promotions like the 4/20 pre-order offer. Today the site verifies each visitor's location and age before showing marketplace features where they're legally available.

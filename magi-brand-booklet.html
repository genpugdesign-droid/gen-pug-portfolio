01Foundation

Magi Vision records the mat, surfaces the moments worth reviewing, and hands them to the coach and athlete. The product is not the detector. The product is the review loop.

Mission

Make combat knowledge more reviewable, transmissible and durable. The dojo should remember more without becoming less human.

Why it exists

Every pose model in production assumes bodies stay separate. Grappling is the sport where they don't.

The loop

Practice happens. The system keeps candidate moments. A coach or athlete reviews them. The system learns which signals mattered.

MACHINE DOES

Memory across every frame · Repetition without fatigue · Surfacing patterns · Comparing attempts over time

HUMANS KEEP

Meaning · Standards · Intent · Pedagogy · Context and care

Audiences

  • Coaches become less limited by attention.
  • Athletes get a correction mirror, not a vanity mirror.
  • Dojos lend their mats: Kano Martial Arts (Manhattan), Krystek Martial Arts (Queens), The Dojo (Brooklyn).
  • Investors and partners see the build running, nothing staged.
  • Health and fitness through muscle-activation education: which muscles fire in a throw, pin or submission.

02Voice

An instrument built by people who train: precise, plain, and never claiming more than the system knows.

From the mat

Use the sport's words correctly: grip, kuzushi, uchi-mata, randori, ground phase. Write as a practitioner, not a spectator.

Short and declarative

"Grappling breaks computer vision." "Nothing here is staged: it is the build, running."

Truthful by design

Copy and visuals never upgrade certainty. "Candidate" stays "candidate" until a detector clears its gate.

Human at the center

AI serves the coach and the athlete. It doesn't replace their judgment.

Mechanics

  • Eyebrows start with //: // DEMO, // DOJOS, // SHOP.
  • Buttons and system tags sit in brackets: [ buy now ], [ NO SIGNAL ].
  • In the software, athletes are P0 and P1. Role labels are ATK and RCV.

03Logo and marks

Lockup v3 is the primary logo: the pentacle mark beside the lowercase magi wordmark. White on dark is the default; black only on light.

Lockup v3 · whitedefault
Lockup v3 · blacklight grounds only
  • Keep mark and wordmark together. Never retype "magi" in a font as a stand-in.
  • Color the lockup only white, black, or the HUD's theme ink. Cyan and red are signal colors, not logo colors.
  • Clear space: at least the height of the i's diamond on every side.
  • In the HUD the lockup is capped at 1.7× the name size.

07Telemetry software

The software's first rule is truth. The visual layer makes the system legible, but it never claims more certainty than the data has.

Audience state words

OPEN ENGAGED ATTACK GROUND RECOVERY LEVEL CHANGE · 1.2 s

Escalation reads as temperature: slate, amber, red.

Surfaces

Operator HUDEverything the recorder computed
Audience TVCalm state word, names, a few meters. Missing fields are not drawn.
DashboardSessions, totals, competitors, signal peaks, selected throw

Layout grammar

  • Numbered panels: COMBAT ANALYSIS 01, PAIR METRICS 02.
  • Label left in dim ink, value right in bold. Missing values are an em dash, never 0.
  • Six meters: Engagement, Entry, Best entry, Knee asym, Support loss, Descent.

Truth rules

  • Athletes are P0 and P1 by default. ATK / RCV only once the receiver is bound.
  • "Candidate" stays "candidate": O-SOTO CANDIDATE score=0.78, not O-SOTO.
  • When in doubt, make the system look better, not the claim stronger.
08Imagery

Real training on real mats, shot the way the system sees it. Nothing staged: the camera on the wall, the gi, the grip.

  • Real sessions only. Label external or reference footage as such.
  • Photos sit inside cards or full-bleed on black; they don't float on white.
  • Credit people and places by name in captions.
  • Get consent from athletes and the dojo before footage goes public.

// how this booklet stays alive

The booklet follows two sources of truth: the live site and the telemetry software. It never leads them.

  1. Change the source first: the color token, the HUD constant, the logo file.
  2. Update the booklet page that covers it.
  3. Log it: one changelog row with the date and what changed.
  4. Unsettled? It stays out of the rules until it's decided.
